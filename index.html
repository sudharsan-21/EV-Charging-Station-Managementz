Infrastructure Operating System

Why EV Charging Management?

Managing EV infrastructure requires more than basic reservations. Our platform unifies charger availability, power delivery, automated scheduling, and maintenance.

Easy Charger Discovery

Filter stations by real-time status, connector type (CCS2, Type 2, CHAdeMO, GB/T), and output capacity from 7.4 kW up to 150 kW.

Conflict-Free Slot Reservation

Integrated time-slot booking engine that checks charger availability dynamically to prevent overlapping reservations.

Real-Time Availability Simulation

Visual status boards reflecting real-time states: Available, Charging, Reserved, Maintenance, or Offline.

Charging Cost Estimation

Accurate charging cost calculations based on battery capacity, delta state of charge, and station electricity tariffs.

Maintenance & Fault Management

Automated technician dispatch and charger auto-lock when under repair to guarantee maximum infrastructure safety.

Usage & Utilization Analytics

Track station throughput, charger utilization percentage, daily kilowatt-hours delivered, and revenue performance.
